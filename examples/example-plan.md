## Transportation

**Getting there**
- Fly into Kansai International (KIX) — round-trip ~$700–900/person from major
  US gateways; book ~2 months out.
- KIX → Kyoto: JR Haruka Limited Express, ~75 min, ~$16 (ICOCA/Haruka combo
  pass recommended).

**Getting around Kyoto**
- No need for a rental car — temples and Gion are walkable and buses are
  excellent. Buy a 1-day bus pass (~$5) for temple-hopping days.
- Taxi for evenings with dinner reservations (~$8–15 per ride in the city).
- Install the Kyoto City Bus app and Google Maps transit layer.

## Daily Itinerary

### Day 1 — Sat Oct 10: Arrival & Gion
- **Afternoon (~3pm):** Arrive KIX, Haruka to Kyoto Station, check in.
- **Late afternoon:** Walk through Gion, stop at Yasaka Shrine.
- **Evening:** Anniversary dinner — Kaiseki at **Roan Kikunoi** (reserve now).
- **Backup:** Gion Kappa for a more casual first night.

### Day 2 — Sun Oct 11: Eastern Temples
- **Morning (8am):** Kiyomizu-dera before crowds (1.5 hr).
- **Late morning:** Walk Sannenzaka/Ninenzaka → Kodai-ji.
- **Lunch:** **Menbaka Fire Ramen** or **Omen** for handmade udon.
- **Afternoon:** Kennin-ji (Zen garden), tea at Ippodo.
- **Evening:** Pontocho alley stroll, dinner at **Gion Karyo**.

### Day 3 — Mon Oct 12: Arashiyama & Splurge Night
- **Morning (8am):** Arashiyama Bamboo Grove + Tenryu-ji garden.
- **Late morning:** Togetsukyo Bridge, riverside walk.
- **Lunch:** **Yudofu Sagano** — tofu kaiseki by the temple.
- **Afternoon:** Iwatayama Monkey Park (light hike) or return to relax.
- **Evening:** Tawaraya ryokan stay; private kaiseki dinner in-room.

### Day 4 — Tue Oct 13: Nishiki & Departure
- **Morning:** Nishiki Market for souvenirs + street food breakfast.
- **Late morning:** Nijo Castle (UNESCO, 1 hr).
- **Lunch:** **Honke Owariya** — 550-year-old soba house.
- **Afternoon:** Haruka back to KIX, evening flight home.

## Practical Notes

- **Visa:** US citizens get 90 days visa-free on arrival; no eVisa needed.
- **Weather:** Mid-Oct highs ~22°C, lows ~14°C, light rain possible. Pack
  layers + a light rain shell.
- **Book now:** Roan Kikunoi, Tawaraya (call or booking form), Kiyomizu-dera
  early-entry if available.
- **Transit:** Get an ICOCA card at KIX; load ¥3,000 to start.
- **Plugs:** Type A (US-compatible), 100V — no adapter needed.
- **Tipping:** Not expected; keep it simple.
- **Anniversary touch:** Reserve a private tea ceremony at Camellia — ~$50
  per person, ~1 hr, very romantic.
